## Compliance notes
- The files are redistributed unmodified. Attribution, source links and license links are provided above.
- If an asset is modified, the modification must be identified and the modified asset distributed under the same or a compatible ShareAlike license.
- The AED diagram is public domain according to its creator's release.
- No endorsement by Wikimedia Commons, the listed artists, AHA or AAP is implied.
- Some illustrations were created before the 2025 guidelines. They are visual aids only; do not infer a current clinical recommendation from an illustration alone.

Run `bash tools/fetch_licensed_images.sh` to download the originals into `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`.
